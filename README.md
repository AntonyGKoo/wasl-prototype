# Wasl prototype

Clickable mockup of Wasl, the networking app that remembers who you met and why it mattered.

- Open the mockup: https://antonygkoo.github.io/wasl-prototype/
- The real app (live, installable): https://wasl-sable.vercel.app
- Walkthrough video: https://antonygkoo.github.io/wasl-prototype/walkthrough.mp4
- Screenshots of every step: `screens/` (`00-all-steps.png` shows them all)

Jump to a step with `#step-1` to `#step-7` or `#step-bonus`. Switch the side panel to **User test** to run the six timed test tasks.

Source lives in the main Wasl repo at `public/mockup/index.html`.
